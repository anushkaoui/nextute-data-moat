The problem

Coaching institutes are flying blind at the moment it matters.

The signals exist. They are trapped in OMR sheets, Excel files and WhatsApp threads.

01 · Signals are fragmented

Tests, attendance and fees live in three to five disconnected tools. No one joins them at the student level.

02 · Action arrives late

Owners see dropout after attendance collapses. Teachers cannot see which topic needs reteaching this week.

03 · Parents get a rank

A percentile without a reason or a next step creates anxiety, not trust — and does not retain the student.

The moat is not more content. It is the workflow that turns existing data into timely, explainable action.

AI / ML design

AI where it earns its place.

Explainable statistics first. Learned models where the data supports them. Rules where it does not.

Measure
Rasch / 1PL

Ability and item difficulty from response patterns. Empirical-Bayes shrinkage (κ=8), 120-day half-life, 10 anchor items per test.

Predict
Calibrated disengagement

Logistic model on attendance, score and mastery features. 60/20/20 split, Platt scaling, top-10% review.

Be honest
Rules for thin outcomes

Fee default and falling behind are transparent v1 rules, labelled as rules in the model card.

Communicate
Locked facts + RAG-lite

Template-first report; every number validated. Study plans retrieve curated topic briefs. No student data to an external model.

No student-facing chatbot · No invented numbers · No black-box exam rank

What it does not prove
