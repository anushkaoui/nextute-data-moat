Honest limitations

What the numbers mean
  • All results shown are from a synthetic pilot (3 institutes, 1,000 students, 180,000 responses, seed 20260401).
  • The risk model is trained on a designed disengagement signal; AUC near 0.99 is optimistic and not a field claim.
  • Percentile bands are internal cohort estimates; they are never mapped to a JEE/NEET rank.
  • Falling-behind and fee-default are v1 transparent rules until the institute has 12+ months of outcomes.
Controls in this build
  • Purpose-bound consent ledger; parent reports require an active consent and a personal expiring link.
  • PII stored in a separate table; guardians' phone numbers are HMACed, not stored in clear.
  • Risk reasons are feature contributions, not free text; parent reports never expose risk bands.
  • Import rows are quarantined with errors; commit is idempotent and audited in audit_event.
  • LLM path is off; reports use a reviewed bilingual template with fact locking.
